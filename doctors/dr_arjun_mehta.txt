SERVICES
1. Cardiac Consultation
   Price: 800 rupees, Duration: 30 minutes
   About: Complete heart health evaluation, medical history review, risk assessment
   Good for: Chest pain, shortness of breath, high blood pressure, first time cardiac checkup
   Includes ECG if required

2. ECG (Electrocardiogram)
   Price: 300 rupees, Duration: 15 minutes
   About: Records electrical activity of the heart, detects irregularities
   Good for: Palpitations, chest pain, dizziness, routine heart checkup
   Result: Report available same day

3. Echocardiography
   Price: 2500 rupees, Duration: 30 minutes
   About: Ultrasound of the heart, checks heart valves and pumping function
   Good for: Heart valve issues, heart failure, pre surgery evaluation
   Result: Report within 24 hours

4. Stress Test (TMT)
   Price: 1800 rupees, Duration: 45 minutes
   About: Monitors heart during physical exertion on treadmill
   Good for: Chest pain during activity, checking heart fitness, post heart attack recovery
   Note: Comfortable clothing and shoes required

5. Holter Monitoring
   Price: 2000 rupees, Duration: 24 hours
   About: Continuous ECG recording worn as small device for a full day
   Good for: Irregular heartbeat, palpitations that come and go
   Note: Normal activities can continue while wearing the device

6. Hypertension Management
   Price: 600 rupees, Duration: 20 minutes
   About: Blood pressure monitoring, medication review, lifestyle guidance
   Good for: High blood pressure patients, medication adjustment
   Recommended monthly or as advised
