### 6. Recommendations for Improvement

To elevate this experience from "good" to "exceptional," we recommend the following adjustments:

* **Integrate Calendar Read/Write Capabilities:** The single biggest upgrade for Aria would be integrating her with the clinic’s scheduling software. Allowing her to authenticate the user and process rescheduling/cancellations directly would turn this 2-minute "redirect" call into a 30-second "fully resolved" call.
* **Proactive Information Gathering:** If Aria cannot perform the reschedule directly, she should gather the necessary details *before* handing the task to the human team. 
  * *Example:* "I can't reschedule that directly, but I can take down your preferences. What day and time work best for you? I'll pass this straight to our team so they have it ready when they call you."
* **Smart Clarification Protocols:** To handle ambiguous phrases like *"can you cancel my call?"*, Aria should be trained to ask a quick clarifying question rather than delivering a generic rejection message. 
  * *Example:* "Just to make sure I understand—did you want to cancel your upcoming appointment with Dr. Mehta, or did you want to cancel the callback request I just put in for you?"
